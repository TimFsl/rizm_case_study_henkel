## Claims with no retained URL

These notes are in `config/assumptions.yaml` or in `CASE_STUDY.md`. No publication URL was retained in this repository, so they have no source ID and are not verified facts.

- Late-2023 fuel-mix interpretation: about 80% gaseous fuels and 20% coal, with about 40% of the gaseous share biogenic. The 68% / 32% / 0% coal split is the screening assumption built from that interpretation. BIO01 does not verify it.
- 2024 coal phase-out, and the choice to leave backup light fuel oil out of normal dispatch.
- StoREN scenario grid connection of about 64 MW.
- Historical steam production used to anchor the steam-demand estimate.
- Historical electricity generation used as the ~260-280 GWh/a screening band.
- Spray-dryer instrumentation, including any count above 50 sensors, and spray-dryer heat recovery at Düsseldorf or at other Henkel plants.
